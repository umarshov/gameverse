Play smarter

PC Gaming Essentials

Useful starting points for a smoother experience.

⚡

FPS & smoothness

Use a suitable resolution, cap unstable frame rates if needed, close unnecessary background apps and monitor temperatures.

🖥️

Know your hardware

Compare your CPU, GPU and RAM with the official requirements. An SSD can improve loading but does not replace a capable GPU.

🛡️

Play safely

Download games and updates from official stores. Avoid suspicious installers, cheats and files claiming unrealistic performance boosts.
